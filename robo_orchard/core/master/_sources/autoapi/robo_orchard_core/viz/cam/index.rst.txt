.. py:class:: CameraMixin

   The camera mixin class.

   The camera mixin class provides the basic interface for camera
   visualization. It is used to render the camera view and get the
   camera pose in the world frame.

   The camera move mixin class.

   The camera move mixin class provides the basic interface for camera
   movement.

   :param local_coord_axis: The local coordinate axis of the camera.
   :type local_coord_axis: CoordAxis


   .. py:method:: move_forward(amount: float, move_scale: float)

      Move the camera in the local forward direction.

      The distance of the move is proportional to the scale of the move:

      .. code-block:: python

          distance = math.exp(math.log(move_scale) + amount) - move_scale


      :param amount: The amount to move forward.
      :type amount: float
      :param move_scale: The scale of the move.
      :type move_scale: float



   .. py:method:: move_translation(amount_down: float, amount_right: float, move_scale: float)

      Move the camera in the local up and right directions.

      The distance of the move is proportional to the scale of the move:

      .. code-block:: python

              distance = move_scale * amount

      :param amount_down: The amount to move down.
      :type amount_down: float
      :param amount_right: The amount to move right.
      :type amount_right: float
      :param move_scale: The scale of the move.
      :type move_scale: float



   .. py:method:: first_person_view_rot(pitch_down: float, yaw_right: float)

      Rotate the camera in the local pitch and yaw directions.

      :param pitch_down: The radians to pitch down.
      :type pitch_down: float
      :param yaw_right: The radians to yaw right.
      :type yaw_right: float
