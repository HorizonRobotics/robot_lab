.. py:function:: project_points_to_image(points_3d: torch.Tensor, projection_mat: torch.Tensor) -> torch.Tensor

   Project 3D points to 2D image plane using the camera intrinsic matrix.

   :param points_3d: A tensor of shape (P, 3) or (B, P, 3)
                     representing the 3D points in any coordinate frame.
   :type points_3d: torch.Tensor
   :param projection_mat: A tensor of shape (3, 3) or (B, 3, 3),
                          or (3, 4) or (B, 3, 4) , or (4, 4) or (B, 4, 4) representing
                          projection matrix.
   :type projection_mat: torch.Tensor

   :returns:

             A tensor of shape (B, P, 3) representing the projected
                 2D points with depth (u, v, depth) in the image plane. The depth
                 is orthogonal distance from the camera's image plane to the
                 3D point.
   :rtype: torch.Tensor


.. py:function:: unproject_image_points(uvd: torch.Tensor, to_image_proj: torch.Tensor | None = None, from_image_unpoj: torch.Tensor | None = None) -> torch.Tensor

   Unproject 2D image points with depth to 3D points in camera frame.

   :param uvd: A tensor of shape (P, 3) or (B, P, 3)
               representing the 2D image points with depth (u, v, depth).
   :type uvd: torch.Tensor
   :param to_image_proj: A tensor of shape (3, 3)
                         or (B, 3, 3), or (3, 4) or (B, 3, 4) , or (4, 4) or (B, 4, 4)
                         representing projection matrix to image plane. If
                         projection_mat has shape (4, 4) or (B, 4, 4), the last row should
                         be [0, 0, 0, 1], as the denominator of the 4D homogeneous
                         coordinate is unknown.
   :type to_image_proj: torch.Tensor | None
   :param from_image_unpoj: The inverse of the projection
                            matrix from camera frame to image plane. If provided, it will be
                            used instead of computing the inverse of to_image_proj.
   :type from_image_unpoj: torch.Tensor | None

   :returns:

             A tensor of shape (B, P, 3) representing the unprojected
                 3D points in camera frame.
   :rtype: torch.Tensor
