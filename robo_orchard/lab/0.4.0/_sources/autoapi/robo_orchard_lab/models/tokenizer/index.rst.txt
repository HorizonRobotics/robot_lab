tokenizer
=========

.. py:module:: robo_orchard_lab.models.tokenizer

.. autoapi-nested-parse::

   Tokenizer is a mapping of data to discrete tokens.

   A tokenizer usually provides methods to encode and decode data. So it can be
   regarded as a special type of codec.
