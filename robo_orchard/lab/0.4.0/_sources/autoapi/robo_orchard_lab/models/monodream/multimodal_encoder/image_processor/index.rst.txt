      Padding function.

      Pad an image to `(pad_size["height"], pad_size["width"])` to
      the right and bottom.


      :param image: Image to pad.
      :type image: `np.ndarray`
      :param pad_size: Size of the output image after padding.
      :type pad_size: `Dict[str, int]`
      :param data_format: The data format of the image.

      :type data_format: `str` or `ChannelDimension`, *optional*
