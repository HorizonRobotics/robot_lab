   .. py:attribute:: invert_rgb
      :type:  bool
      :value: False


      Whether to invert RGB to BGR.

      For some datasets, the actual image data may be incorrectly stored as RGB
      but should be treated as BGR. This flag allows to invert the channels
      accordingly.
